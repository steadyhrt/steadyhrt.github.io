Perimenopause

Yes, you can still get pregnant

How long contraception matters, and why HRT doesn't count.

NHS guidance

How long to keep using it

  • Age 50 or over: for 1 year after your final period
  • Age 40 to 49: for 2 years after your final period
Important

HRT is not contraception

UK NICE guidance says women should be told that HRT doesn't prevent pregnancy.

The tricky part

Hidden signs

  • The mini pill, hormonal coil, implant and injection can make periods irregular or stop them
  • The combined pill gives a monthly bleed for as long as you take it
  • So it can be hard to tell where you are in menopause
Bring this to your appointment

Questions for your doctor

  • Which contraception suits me at this stage?
  • How will we know when I've reached menopause?
  • Does my current method work alongside HRT?
