SPOTTING MONTHS INTO HRT

Spotting a few months in. Normal?

The NHS says it's common early on, and not usually a sign of anything serious.

What counts as expected

It depends on your type of HRT (NHS)

  • NHS, sequential HRT: bleeding at the end of each progestogen course is normal
  • NHS, continuous combined HRT: irregular spotting is common in the first 4 to 6 months
Around 3 months in

Still spotting around 3 months in?

NHS guidance places the first HRT review at 3 months and says to mention irregular bleeding then. If it doesn't go away, it notes a doctor might suggest changing your progestogen dose.

The timeline to know

6 months after starting HRT

The NHS says irregular bleeding that carries on more than 6 months after you start HRT is worth a doctor visit.

When to get it checked

The NHS says to see a doctor if

  • Your bleeding becomes heavier
  • You bleed after a while with no periods
Bring this to your appointment

Questions for your doctor

  • Is the bleeding I'm having expected for my type of HRT?
  • What should I track about the bleeding before my next appointment?
  • What would make you want to look into it further?
