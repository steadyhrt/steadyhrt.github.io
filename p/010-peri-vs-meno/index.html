The basics

Peri, meno, post: what's the difference?

Three words, three stages.

Definitions

The three stages

  • Perimenopause: the lead-up, often starting with changing periods
  • Menopause: reached after 12 months with no period
  • Postmenopause: the time after that
How long symptoms last

7–9 yrs

The usual span of menopause symptoms. Sometimes it's longer.

Symptoms shift

It doesn't stay the same

  • Hot flashes and night sweats may ease off
  • Then low mood or anxiety can take their place
  • Joint pain and vaginal dryness can carry on after menopause

Questions for your doctor

  • Which of my symptoms could be hormonal?
  • What are my options at this stage?
