The FDA's HRT warning change

What changed on US hormone therapy labels, and what didn't.

What changed

The black box is going

  • The FDA began removing the broad boxed warnings from menopause HRT
  • Label language on heart disease, breast cancer and probable dementia is being taken out
What stayed

One warning remains

  • Systemic estrogen-alone products keep the boxed warning for endometrial cancer (the uterine lining)
Why now

The original study

  • The warnings followed the Women's Health Initiative study in the early 2000s
  • Participants were 63 on average, a decade or more past typical menopause
  • They took a hormone formulation that's rarely used today
Timing

New label guidance

The labels will recommend starting systemic HRT before age 60, or within 10 years of when menopause began. When to start, and for how long, stays a decision between you and your prescriber.

Questions for your doctor

  • Given my age and history, how do benefits and risks look for me?
  • Which form of HRT would suit me?
