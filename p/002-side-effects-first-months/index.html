Starting HRT

Side effects in the first months

What's common, what usually settles, and when to call your doctor.

With estrogen

Commonly reported

  • Headaches or tender breasts
  • Nausea, leg cramps or a mild rash
  • Spotting or unexpected bleeding
With progestogen

Commonly reported

  • Changes to periods, or spotting
  • Feeling tired or dizzy, or acne
  • Low mood or mood swings
Good to know

Often mild, often temporary

Plenty of people get no side effects at all. When they do show up, they often ease within a few weeks. Some look a lot like menopause symptoms themselves.

The 3-month mark

Give it time

  • The NHS says side effects tend to improve, and suggests giving treatment around 3 months where possible
  • Your doctor may adjust the type, the dose or the form, e.g. tablets to a patch
Call your doctor if

Don't wait it out

  • Side effects feel severe
  • They're still there after 3 months
  • You're not sure what's causing a symptom
