APPOINTMENT COMING UP

Appointment soon and can't recall a thing?

Your mind goes blank in the room. Here's a tick-list of what to note and bring.

TICK: PERIODS AND BLEEDING

Track your cycle changes

  • Periods closer together, further apart, lighter or heavier
  • The date of your last period
  • Any bleeding after 12 months with no period. The NHS says it needs checking
TICK: HEAT, SLEEP AND MOOD

Write down the daily stuff

  • Hot flashes or night sweats: when, and how long they last
  • Trouble falling asleep or getting back to sleep
  • Mood swings, anxiety or feeling more irritable
  • Memory or concentration slips
TICK: BODY CHANGES

Note the ones that feel awkward to say

  • Vaginal dryness, burning or itching, or sex that hurts
  • More UTIs, or needing to pee more, including at night
  • Joint and muscle aches, or headaches worse than usual
  • A noticeable or racing heartbeat
TICK: WHAT TO BRING

Details that shape the conversation

  • Any hormonal contraception, since it can blur the picture
  • Whether your symptoms have shifted over time
  • Family and medical history, plus what you prefer
  • On HRT in perimenopause? Note any irregular or heavier bleeding or spotting. The NHS says to talk to a GP if it's still happening after 6 months
Bring this to your appointment

Questions for your doctor

  • Could what I've noted point to perimenopause or menopause?
  • What are my options for the symptoms that bother me most?
  • Does my family or medical history change what suits me?
