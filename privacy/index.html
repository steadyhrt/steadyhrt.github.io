Privacy

This website does not use cookies or advertising trackers. The only personal data we collect is your email address if you join the waitlist. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical data (such as IP addresses) for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.

YouTube API Services

SteadyHRT uses YouTube API Services through an internal publishing tool ("SteadyHRT Publisher"). By using our YouTube channel or this tool you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

  • What the tool accesses: only the SteadyHRT YouTube channel that we own. It uploads our own videos (videos.insert) and reads basic statistics of our own channel and videos (channels.list, videos.list).
  • What it does not do: it does not access, collect, store or share any information about other YouTube users or channels, and it is not offered to anyone outside SteadyHRT.
  • Storage: the only credential stored is an OAuth refresh token for our own channel, kept on our own computer and never shared. View statistics of our own videos are kept for up to 12 months to improve our content, then deleted.
  • Revoking access and deletion: access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page; when access is revoked we delete the stored token and statistics within 7 days. Questions or deletion requests: steadyhrt.app@gmail.com.

Other publishing tools

We also use the official Meta (Instagram, Facebook, Threads) and Pinterest APIs only to publish our own posts to our own SteadyHRT accounts and read their statistics. These tools do not access other people's data.

If you join our waitlist, we store your email address (and which post or platform you came from) with our email provider, Brevo, and use it only to send you SteadyHRT updates. You can unsubscribe at any time from any email, or ask us to delete your data at steadyhrt.app@gmail.com.
