Cross Stitch Pattern Tracker for iPhone

Open the PDF pattern you bought. Tap a colour. See every stitch of it, and tick them off as you go.

Rose sampler · DMC 3354 highlighted
3354: 18 of 32 stitches done
  • Highlight one colourTap a thread in the key and every matching square lights up. No more hunting for that one symbol.
  • Mark stitches doneSwipe over what you have stitched. A counter shows what is left, per colour and for the whole pattern.
  • Never lose your placeZoom, pan and pick up exactly where you stopped, even on a 20-page chart.
  • Your files stay yoursPatterns stay on your phone. Nothing is uploaded, shared or converted.

Planned to read PDF charts made with common chart programs (PCStitch, WinStitch/MacStitch, Pattern Maker). Scanned paper charts will not work.
