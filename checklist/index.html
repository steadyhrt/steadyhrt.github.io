Doctor-visit checklist for HRT

Bring this to your next appointment about perimenopause, menopause or HRT. Download the PDF

Bring with you

  • Your current HRT: which form (patch, gel, spray, tablet, vaginal), product name, and when you started
  • Notes from the last few weeks: hot flashes and night sweats, sleep, mood, and anything new
  • Dates of any bleeding or spotting
  • Any side effects, and when they began
  • Your history of blood clots, stroke or breast cancer, including in close family
  • When your last mammogram was

Questions you could ask

  • Are my symptoms improving as expected?
  • Is my bleeding pattern expected for my type of HRT?
  • Would a different form of HRT (patch, gel, spray or tablet) suit me better?
  • Do I have risk factors that affect which HRT is right for me?
  • Do I still need contraception?
  • Could vaginal estrogen help with vaginal symptoms?
  • When should we review my treatment again?

Contact your doctor sooner if

  • You have any vaginal bleeding after 12 months or more without a period
  • Irregular bleeding continues more than 6 months after starting HRT, or bleeding gets heavier
  • Side effects are severe, or last longer than 3 months
