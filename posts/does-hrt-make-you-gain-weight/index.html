Myth vs fact

Does HRT make you gain weight?

A common worry, and what the evidence says.

The evidence

Probably not the HRT

  • There's little evidence that most types of HRT cause weight gain
  • Weight often shifts with menopause and age, with or without HRT
Menopause itself

Where it tends to go

  • Weight gain in perimenopause and menopause is common
  • It often settles around the stomach and upper body
What helps

Move, and eat well

Regular exercise and a balanced diet help manage weight, and exercise also helps keep muscles strong as you age.

Bring this to your appointment

Questions for your doctor

  • Could my weight changes be menopause-related?
  • What kind of activity would help my bones and muscles?
